Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about bidding rules, position retention, and billing cycles.

When another entity places a qualifying outbid (minimum increment of +₹1,999/mo for developers or +₹999/mo for partners), the new bidder takes your previous rank, and your entity automatically shifts down by exactly one position (e.g. from #1 to #2). You remain visible on the board and receive an instant SMS/WhatsApp notification giving you the immediate opportunity to counter-bid.
Bids are placed on a 30-day billing cycle. At the end of each cycle, the holding entity can renew at their existing bid rate to maintain their position, provided they have not been outbid during the active month. Payments can be settled seamlessly via UPI or NEFT with instant automated digital invoicing.
