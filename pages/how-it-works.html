How the board works

No algorithms decide who is recommended. Position is bought and the price is public, so buyers always know what they are seeing.

Step 1

Developer places a bid

Any RERA-certified developer can bid for a position on the leaderboard. The highest bid at any time holds rank #1.

Step 2

Position — and price — go public

We display the bid amount and the exact date each developer or partner took their position. Anyone can verify it at a glance.

Step 3

Outbid anytime

A lower-ranked developer can retake the top spot by paying more than the current leader. The board updates immediately.
