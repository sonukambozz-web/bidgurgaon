How onboarding works

No long applications. No waiting in a queue.

One rule governs the whole board: the highest bid holds the top position — and the first phone call.

Step 1

Share your RERA channel partner license

Call or WhatsApp our onboarding desk with your Haryana RERA channel-partner registration number. We verify it against the public RERA portal before anything else happens.

Step 2

Pick your projects and bid

Choose which live projects you want to represent, and set your position bid for the main leaderboard and for each project page. We confirm your ranking, in writing, before you pay.

Step 3

Go live, phone number and all

Your name, badge and phone number go live on the board and on every project page you've chosen. Any partner can outbid you later — you'll get notified before you're displaced.
