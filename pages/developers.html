Transparency notice

What "position bid" means

Rank on this board is not an editorial judgement about build quality, delivery track record, or trustworthiness — it reflects only what a developer has agreed to pay for visibility, and for how long they've held that position. We publish the figure and the date openly so buyers can weigh it accordingly. Before making a purchase decision, verify a project's RERA registration independently on the Haryana RERA portal and visit the site in person.
